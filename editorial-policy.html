Editorial Policy

Our pages are designed to help readers make informed software decisions.

How we research

  • We begin with official product pages, pricing and terms.
  • We compare tools by real use cases, not only feature lists.
  • We separate confirmed facts from our editorial judgment.
  • We update pages when pricing, features or availability changes.

AI-assisted writing

We may use AI for research organization, drafts and formatting. Human review, source checking and editorial judgment are required before publication. We do not publish mass-produced pages simply to target keywords.
