A reliable AI video workflow

Strong results come from a clear production process, not from repeating random prompts.

  1. 1. Define the deliverableChoose the platform, aspect ratio, duration, audience and call to action before generation.
  2. 2. Build a visual referenceStart with a clean key frame, product image, character sheet or concise shot description.
  3. 3. Generate and compareTest short variations, change one variable at a time and select the strongest motion.
  4. 4. Finish for publishingEdit timing, add captions and licensed audio, then export at the resolution your channel needs.
