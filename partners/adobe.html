Firefly AI

One place to explore, edit and finish.

Adobe Firefly brings image, video, audio, vector and editing workflows into one creative space. You can start with a prompt, refine the result and move into the Adobe app that fits the job.

  • Generate images, video, vectors, sound effects and speech.
  • Use controls for style, lighting, color and composition.
  • Move results into Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere or Express.
  • Keep commercial-use questions visible before publishing.

BEFORE YOU PAY

What Adobe is best at

Adobe makes the most sense when you want AI inside a mature editing and delivery system — not just a one-off generated image.

  • Repeatable brand and campaign work
  • Detailed editing after generation
  • Connected photo, design and video workflows
  • Formats and handoff for real clients
MAHGPT LEARNING PATH

Who should consider it?

Designers, photographers, editors, marketers and teams that value control, established tools and a workflow that can survive past the first experiment.

  • Start with a real project
  • Check credits, plan limits and exports
  • Compare time saved after the learning curve
  • Review commercial-use terms for your use case
