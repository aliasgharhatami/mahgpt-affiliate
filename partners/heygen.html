A useful production loop

From consent to localized cut

Four decisions make the difference between a novelty avatar and a usable communication system.

01

Pick the face

Choose a stock presenter, create a digital twin or animate an approved photo. Use consent and rights as the first filter.

02

Write the delivery

Paste the script or upload a deck. Decide the audience, tone, language, frame and length before generating.

03

Direct the scene

Adjust visuals, captions, background, pacing and branding in the studio. Treat the first render as an editable draft.

04

Localize and publish

Translate, review terminology and export versions for the market, platform or learning system that needs them.

HeyGen’s official translator supports uploading a video or pasting a YouTube link, then choosing a target language. Read the video translator guide before using it for client or commercial work.
