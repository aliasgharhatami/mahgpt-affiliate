Where Descript shines

Descript is strongest when the spoken word is central to the story: interviews, podcasts, tutorials, courses, webinars and social clips.

  • Fast first cuts without wrestling with a timeline
  • Repurposing one recording into multiple formats
  • Creators who prefer writing to traditional editing
  • Teams that need comments, scenes and simple publishing

Underlord and AI-assisted editing

Descript is extending its text-first editor with an AI collaborator that can read the project, suggest edits and handle repetitive production tasks.

  • Use AI for speed without losing editorial control
  • Check transcription and voice results before export
  • Review plan limits for media minutes and AI credits
  • Keep a human pass for tone, facts and privacy
