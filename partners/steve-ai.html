A repeatable production loop

From rough thought to finished cut

Steve AI’s own text-to-video guidance describes a three-step start; this is the useful editorial version for a real workflow.

1

Bring the brief

Paste a script, blog, prompt, notes, audio file or image. Start with the idea, not a blank timeline.

2

Choose the voice

Select the style, format, duration, language, presenter or visual direction for the audience.

3

Direct the scenes

Review the generated storyboard, replace visuals, adjust text, add motion and apply your brand kit.

4

Export and repeat

Publish to YouTube or export versions for social, training, ads and internal communication.
