A useful Semrush loop

One project, four decisions. Keep the human judgment in the room.

01 · DISCOVER

What is worth winning?

Start with the audience, market and a seed topic. Use keyword and market research to see real demand.

02 · COMPARE

Who already owns it?

Inspect competing pages, intent and gaps. Decide whether you can add a clearer, more useful answer.

03 · BUILD

What should ship?

Turn the evidence into a brief, an on-page plan, a technical fix list or a campaign hypothesis.

04 · MEASURE

Did the move work?

Track rankings, visibility, traffic and AI mentions. Refresh the plan when the market changes.
