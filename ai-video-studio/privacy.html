Privacy & Media Handling — Beta

Limited beta notice. This document covers email login and text-only draft storage for authorized testers, not public customer registration, media uploads or paid editing. The production legal terms will be finalized before public launch.

Current limited beta

MAH GPT Studio currently offers test sign-in for email addresses authorized in the connected Supabase project's team, and allows those signed-in users to save text-only project drafts. Customer photo, video, audio uploads, paid rendering, quotes, and Google sign-in are not currently enabled. Please do not enter sensitive personal information in test briefs.

Information processed

During beta, Supabase Auth processes the email address used for sign-in, authentication events, security metadata such as IP address, and necessary session identifiers. We also store text drafts, project titles, selected style references, agreement version and acceptance time for signed-in test users.

Purposes and hosting

We process the information to authenticate beta testers, protect their accounts, save their drafts and troubleshoot the service. The database project is hosted on Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany; Supabase and the email-delivery provider may process authentication and operational metadata under their own applicable terms.

Your choices and rights

Do not use this beta if you are uncomfortable with these limited uses. Project drafts can be deleted through My Projects. To request account deletion, access or correction of personal information, contact info@mahgpt.com. We may delete test data when the beta ends. Exact production retention schedules and additional provider disclosures will be published before public registration.

Media rights

Your media and drafts remain yours, subject to third-party rights. The beta does not upload media to the server, and no submitted drafts will be published for advertising, shared publicly as portfolio pieces or used for training models without a separately requested permission.

Operator and future release

MAH GPT Studio is currently operated as a pre-launch project; contact info@mahgpt.com for data-handling questions. Formal business/controller identity details, rights procedures, applicable law and international transfer disclosures must be finalized before general customer registration opens.
