Privacy & Media Handling

Pre-launch draft. MAH GPT Studio accounts and server-side uploads are not yet active. This page describes our intended approach and is not a final privacy notice or legal agreement. Complete, jurisdiction-specific terms will be published before registration, uploads or payments are enabled.

What you may share

Once the service is launched, you may choose to provide an email address, a Google sign-in identity, project briefs, photos, video, audio and reference links to create or edit your own projects.

Why we would process your files

Uploaded materials would be used only as needed to create, edit, preview, store and deliver the projects you request, and to operate and secure the service. Processing may involve selected cloud hosting, storage, AI and rendering service providers as described in the final notice.

Your content and public sharing

You retain ownership of the media you provide, subject to the rights of any third-party creators. Submission of a project is not permission for us to feature your content in advertisements, a public sample gallery or AI training. Any optional showcase or other secondary use will require a separate choice.

Access and retention

Project media will be private to the account unless you explicitly share it or a lawful disclosure applies. We intend to provide controls for deletion and project management. Before the service goes live, we will publish specific retention periods, storage locations, service providers and relevant transfer safeguards.

Legal disclosures still being finalized

The final notice must identify the legal data controller, contact address, applicable legal bases, any international data transfers, recipients and how users can exercise their rights. These details are not yet finalized; this draft must not be relied on as the live notice.
