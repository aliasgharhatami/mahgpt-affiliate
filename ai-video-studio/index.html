One creative workflow. Multiple editing styles.

Our long-term goal is to make high-quality editing accessible without demanding traditional editing expertise.

01 · REFERENCE

Inspired by your example

Supply a reference video and your own footage. The intended workflow analyzes its visual language, timing and presentation style to produce a tailored edit.

02 · CONVERSATION

Direct edits with words

Ask for a faster opening, different captions, a tighter crop or more expressive graphics. The project should remain editable, not just an exported MP4.

03 · VISUALS

Context-aware visual elements

Combine footage with motion typography, diagrams, relevant retrieved images, and eventually AI-generated imagery where appropriate.

04 · AUDIO

Sound meets motion

Planned tools for transitions, sound effects, music and timing that complement the story rather than overwhelm it.

05 · LANGUAGES

Multilingual by design

Persian, Turkish, English and Arabic are priority languages, including right-to-left typography and speech-aligned captions.

06 · ACCESS

Made for the web

Our intended public release is a cloud-hosted web experience, accessible from computers and mobile browsers, with Android planned later.

What exists today — and what comes next

The project has a working local rendering prototype. We distinguish features demonstrated in experiments from capabilities that are still in development.

Built and tested locally

  • Manifest-driven editing and Remotion-based rendering
  • Animated Persian captions, motion typography and presenter reframing
  • Selected conceptual graphics and subject-background compositing
  • Initial speech alignment and visual-asset retrieval experiments
  • Integrated short-form editorial video prototypes

On the roadmap

  • Open-ended AI-directed editing from user prompts
  • Reliable style analysis from reference videos
  • AI image-generation and broader media retrieval
  • Long-form and multi-footage editing with audio effects
  • Secure multi-user cloud hosting, web editing UI and Android access
●
Current stage: private development / pre-launch

No public upload service, user accounts, or paid subscription is available yet. Prototype demonstrations are available upon request.

Exploring a better AI connection experience

We are designing the editor to work with user-selected AI providers rather than locking editing intelligence to one model.

Connection options we aim to support

We are exploring eligible, officially authorized ChatGPT account connections alongside a separate bring-your-own API key option. Any plan-based access would depend on the provider's permissions and supported capabilities.

Why it matters

The AI can help interpret editing requests and suggest structured timelines, captions and visual assets; the rendering engine carries out the edit. We are not claiming that ChatGPT subscription access is currently integrated or approved.

AI Video Studio is an independent, pre-incorporation project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or presently partnered with OpenAI or Google.
