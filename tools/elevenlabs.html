What is ElevenLabs?

ElevenLabs is an AI audio platform covering text-to-speech, speech-to-text, voice design, voice cloning, dubbing, sound effects, conversational agents and generative audio. It is useful for creators, filmmakers, podcasters, game developers, educators and teams building voice experiences.

Quick verdict: ElevenLabs is most interesting when you need natural-sounding speech, multilingual delivery or a complete audio workflow rather than a single novelty voice.

AgentsBuild conversational experiences that work across voice and chat channels.

Voice cloning: what beginners should know

ElevenLabs distinguishes between Instant Voice Cloning and Professional Voice Cloning. The best result is not only about audio quality: you must have permission to clone the voice, understand the provider’s safety requirements and check whether your intended use is commercially allowed.

For a first test, use your own voice, record clean audio and compare difficult words, names, pauses and emotional delivery. Do not judge a voice from one short sentence.
