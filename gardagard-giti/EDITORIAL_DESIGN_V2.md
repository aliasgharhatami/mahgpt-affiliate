# Gerde Giti — editorial design v2 (approved reference)

## Creative direction (2026-10-25)
- Official Persian brand: **گرد گیتی**
- Exact tagline on ALL formats: **فالو کن تا بگردیم به گرد گیتی**
- Style: **minimalist editorial / contemporary magazine**. The approved reference uses warm ivory/beige vs charcoal black, thin editorial rules, abundant negative space, large Persian typography, a photo partly under a toned panel, and modest local-news metadata.
- Split the photograph into tinted (sepia/black-and-white) and full-color sections. Vary image/text balance across posts within a recognizable grid.
- Keep the country flag and the location visible but small.
- Never add airplanes, travel icons, gold filigree, glowing ornamental borders, 3D globe logos, or other travel-agency motifs.
- For headline: strong Persian sans serif; for supporting body: restrained readable Persian font. Match title's line breaks to each story; enforce exact tagline text and protect its placement in Instagram safe zones.
- Feed: 1080x1350; reel and story: 1080x1920.
- Planned posts per day: **3 reels + 2 photo posts**, with the photo posts also offered as Stories.
- Previously committed navy/gold/teal experimental renderer is **superseded** by this editorial direction and must NOT become the publishing renderer without replacement.
