Privacy & data protection

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 November 2026 · Applies to mahgpt.com and MAH GPT Studio's account features

What is available today: MAH GPT Studio offers public email/Google sign-in and private text-only project drafts. Actual media uploads, AI video generation, paid checkout, video rendering and portfolio publication are not enabled in the current public beta. This policy separates current processing from what will require new disclosures before launch.

1. Who operates this service?

MAH GPT Studio is the service offered at mahgpt.com, together with the related AI Models editorial area. For questions, data requests, complaints, or deletion requests, email info@mahgpt.com. Additional registered operator and business address details will be published as they are formally established and before commercial video orders are offered.

2. Information we receive

  • Account and sign-in data: email address, account identifiers, login method, authentication/session identifiers, login times and anti-abuse/security logs. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide a verified email address, display name, profile image and Google account identifier through the standard openid, email, and profile scopes. Google authentication does not give us access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts, passwords or Google payment information.
  • Drafts you choose to save: project title, text brief, selected reference style and optional reference URL, creation/change dates, and records of terms acceptance and which version of this privacy notice was presented. Recording delivery of a privacy notice does not mean giving consent for advertising or additional processing. Drafts are private to the signed-in account and are not publicly listed.
  • Technical information: connection/IP information, browser and device metadata, service errors and security events collected by infrastructure and authentication providers as necessary to operate and protect the service.
  • Communications: messages you voluntarily send to our published contact email and any information needed to answer your support or privacy request.

3. How we use information, and why

We use the minimum information needed to register and authenticate accounts, restore login sessions, store and display your own drafts, keep unauthorized users out of private projects, respond to requests, prevent misuse and maintain the website. For account authentication and saved drafts, processing is necessary to provide the beta account functions requested by you. Limited technical/security information is processed to prevent abuse and maintain service integrity on the applicable legitimate-interest or other lawful basis; legally required records are kept only where an actual legal obligation applies. We do not treat delivery of a privacy notice or agreement to our terms as blanket consent for additional uses. If a future, distinct purpose requires consent (such as optional portfolio promotion), we will request that consent separately.

Authentication messages are service communications, not a subscription to marketing emails. We do not use Google profile data or private drafts for advertising targeting, sale to data brokers, or AI model training. We do not publish customers' work or use their faces in promotional materials without a distinct, optional authorization.

4. Google Sign-In and limitations

If you choose Google, Google authenticates you and shares the limited profile information needed to create or identify your MAH GPT Studio account. The resulting identity is processed by Supabase Auth. You may choose email-based sign-in instead. Existing email and Google identities may be linked to one underlying account according to the authentication provider's identity-linking safeguards. We do not request Gmail, Drive, Calendar or other extended Google API access for login.

You can revoke an application's Google access through your Google Account connections. Revoking Google access stops future Google-based authorization but does not by itself erase the account or drafts already stored with MAH GPT Studio; contact us for deletion.

Google Sign-In is strictly an account authentication feature in the current beta. We do not request access to Google Workspace, Gmail, Drive or contacts, and we do not use Google generative AI APIs to create intimate images, photographs or video. Google profile data is not used for the generation of AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII) or to train models.

5. Private drafts, images, video, voice and consent

At present: the beta stores text drafts only. Selecting a local image/audio/video in the interface is not the same as uploading it to our servers; private media upload and rendering are not yet operational.

Before media workflows go live: we will publish revised notices explaining which media types are collected, AI/video-processing providers, geographic processing, expected retention, how to request deletion and any necessary permissions. Customers will retain their original ownership of submitted content, subject to third-party rights, and will authorize only the processing needed for the requested service. Users must have lawful rights and necessary permissions to provide third-party works and to depict or use identifiable people's images, likenesses or voices (especially for commercial or advertising uses).

Permission to process a photo or video will not automatically give MAH GPT Studio permission to post it publicly, put it in a portfolio, use it in promotions or train AI models on it. Any optional promotional or model-training use would require a distinct, clearly described choice. Where withdrawal is legally available, we will explain how to withdraw it. Please do not submit sensitive personal details or material involving children during this text-only beta.

6. Payments and card information

Payments are not currently accepted through the Studio. We do not currently request payment card numbers, CVV codes or bank-login credentials for Studio projects. If checkout is launched, we intend to use a specialized payment processor and hosted secure payment fields. Card numbers and CVVs should be entered only into that processor's secure interface, not into a project brief, an email to us, or this website's text fields. We would receive only the transaction information needed for receipts, order status, refunds and legally required accounting, and we will identify the payment processor before launch.

7. Providers and international processing

We use Supabase for authentication and the private database (the Studio project is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany); Resend to deliver authentication emails; Google when you choose Google Sign-In; and Cloudflare for site delivery and related network/security functions. These service providers process data as necessary to provide their services and may handle technical data in other countries depending on their infrastructure and service terms. Google Fonts, if loaded on some site pages, may also involve requests to Google. We do not disclose private drafts to affiliate marketing partners.

The AI Models/editorial pages may link to external services or contain affiliate links. Following an external link takes you to a third party governed by its own privacy notice. Browsing those pages does not give affiliate partners access to your private Studio projects.

8. Storage, security and retention

We use Supabase authentication and access-control policies that restrict project records to their account owners. No internet service can promise absolute security. Session information may be stored in browser storage or necessary cookies to keep you signed in. Service providers also maintain limited logs and backups according to their operational policies.

Saved beta drafts and account records are normally retained while your account is in use. You can remove drafts using My Projects; contact info@mahgpt.com to request account deletion. Deletion may take additional time for backups, fraud prevention or legal obligations. We will not claim a fixed deletion timetable until we establish and publish an operational retention schedule.

9. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may request access, correction, deletion, information about processing, object to certain uses, or withdraw a consent previously granted. Send a request to info@mahgpt.com. We may verify your identity before discussing or changing an account. You can also sign out and remove drafts using the account interface. Deleting a Google OAuth connection from Google does not automatically delete data at Supabase.

10. Children and sensitive information

The current Studio beta is not intended as a service directed at children. Do not include sensitive medical, financial, biometric, identity-document or other special-category information in project drafts. If you believe a child's information was provided without proper authority, contact us so it can be reviewed.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this page when the product, service providers, law or data practices change. Material changes to media processing or payments will be disclosed before those functions begin; where required, we will ask for fresh consent. The current version date is shown above. For any privacy question email info@mahgpt.com.

This page describes current service practices; it is not a substitute for individualized legal review under applicable data protection laws, including where relevant Türkiye's KVKK and the GDPR.
